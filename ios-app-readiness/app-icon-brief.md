# App Icon Brief

## Status

The approved direction has been rendered as a text-free navy, cyan, and gold connected-data mark. Master and resized PNGs are in `assets/icons/`; the 1024 px asset is installed in the Xcode AppIcon set. Branded launch images use the same mark on the navy field. Final review on a Home Screen and in TestFlight remains a device task.

## Direction

Create a simple square icon that reads clearly at small sizes and connects the existing playground identity to data and Python without copying the website header literally.

## Visual ingredients

- Deep navy or charcoal background.
- One bright cyan data path or connected-node motif.
- One warm gold/orange accent to echo the playground's teaching and notebook cues.
- Optional small grid or three-node structure, kept bold and uncluttered.
- No tiny words, screenshots, or detailed dataset illustrations.

## Acceptance criteria

- Recognisable at Home Screen size and in TestFlight.
- Strong contrast in both light and dark surroundings.
- Works without a transparent/white border being required.
- Supplied as an Xcode/Icon Composer-ready app-icon asset set.
- Does not rely on the website's navigation-card artwork as the app icon.
