Privacy policy

This policy explains how the Data Science Python Playground website and the Data Playground iPhone/iPad app handle information.

Effective date: August 31, 2026 · Last updated: September 2, 2026

The service is designed to be free and usable without an account. Your analysis work is intended to stay on your device unless you deliberately share or export it.

1. Who is responsible

Data Science Python Playground is maintained by Elijah Ang.

Privacy and support contact:

2. Information the service may process

When you use the playground, it may process or create:

  • Bundled CSV files, dataset values, Python code, notebook inputs, model settings, charts, and generated results.
  • Temporary in-memory working state needed to keep the current screen functioning.
  • Messages and contact details that you voluntarily send to support.

The current design processes bundled datasets and in-session analysis locally in the browser or app web view. They are not sent to a project-operated server by default. This will be re-checked whenever the native app or a third-party SDK changes.

3. How information is used

Information is used to run data-analysis and machine-learning examples, display tables and charts, provide learning guidance, save or export a result when you request it, and respond to support messages.

The current product does not require user accounts and does not include analytics or cross-app tracking.

4. Storage and retention

Active analysis work remains only in temporary device or browser memory. It may remain available while the app process stays alive in the foreground or background, but terminating or swiping away the app starts a fresh canvas. Files you save or export are controlled by the browser, Files app, or system destination you choose. The project does not provide notebook history, cloud accounts, or project-operated storage for analysis work.

Please do not enter confidential, regulated, or personally identifying information unless you are authorised to process it.

5. Network requests and third parties

The browser version uses local fonts but may request the Python runtime and package resources from third-party hosts. It may also open external dataset-source links. Those providers may receive ordinary technical request information, such as an IP address and browser or device information, under their own policies.

The native app is intended to bundle or reliably cache required runtime assets and should not depend on the live GitHub Pages URL. External websites opened from source links remain subject to their own privacy policies.

6. Advertising

Advertising is not enabled on the current website or native build. Google AdMob has been selected for a possible future app release, initially limited to non-blocking banner placements. The SDK will not be added until the account, test ad units, consent flow, and final data disclosures are configured.

Curated dataset contents, Python code, model inputs, and learning activity should not be used to target advertisements. If an ad SDK later introduces identifiers or personalised advertising, the app will add the required permission and consent choices before enabling that behaviour.

7. Sharing and sale

We do not intentionally sell or rent analysis data. You may choose to share an exported file or chart through the browser, Files app, or system share sheet. That sharing is controlled by you and the destination you select.

8. Children

The service is intended as a general-audience educational tool and is not specifically directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If the audience or advertising configuration changes, this policy and the store disclosures will be reviewed.

9. Privacy questions and requests

For questions about this policy or a privacy/support request, email elijahang77@gmail.com. Please include enough context for us to understand the request. Applicable law may affect what can be provided.

10. Security

Reasonable measures will be used to protect the project and its release infrastructure. No internet-connected software can guarantee absolute security. Keep your device and operating system updated, and only use data you are authorised to process.

11. Changes to this policy

We will update this page when the service’s data flows, native dependencies, advertising setup, persistence model, or support process changes. The effective date above will be updated when a revised policy is published.

This page is the initial public policy copy for the GitHub Pages site. It will be reviewed again before the native app is submitted to the App Store and whenever advertising is enabled.
